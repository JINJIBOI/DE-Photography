About Me

Learn more about me and my background!

Hi, I'm Dallin Everitt

I'm a passionate photographer with a love for capturing moments that tell a story. Along my journey of this artform, I have grown and learned a lot about the craft and the art of photography. One thing I've learned is that it is important to make clients comfortable and to make sure they are having fun during their session. I want to make sure that my clients have a great experience and that they are happy with the final product. I offer a variety of services including family, couples, engagement, senior, portrait, wedding, commercial, and more. I am always looking for new opportunities to grow and learn as a photographer, so if you have any requests for services not listed, please don't hesitate to reach out and I will get back to you as soon as possible. If you are interested in working with me or have any questions, please feel free to reach out to me through the contact form below. I look forward to hearing from you!
